PetTrack turns the phones you already own into a pet monitor: one old phone becomes the camera, this app is where you watch, and everything runs on a server at home. No subscription, no cloud, no account — your pet's video never leaves your house.

WHAT YOU GET

Live view that stays up
· Watch your pet in real time and call them with a sound when you need to
· Remote night mode for dark rooms
· The stream reconnects by itself — a Wi-Fi blip never kills the view

Events that find you
· Motion, sound (bark / meow) and zone events, each with a snapshot
· Alerts on your phone, plus optional remote alerts via ntfy so you hear
  about it at school or at work
· Quiet hours and an arming schedule: only watch when you're away

Evidence, not guesswork
· Replay timeline: a day strip with event dots — tap a dot, watch the clip
· Tap through from event to snapshot to the exact moment in the replay
· Daily recap: the highlight of your pet's day
· Weekly insights: zone time, rest versus active

Built for daily life
· Zones in plain words — "the bed", "the door" — not engineering knobs
· Multi-camera: several old phones, one dashboard
· Home-screen widget and a Wear OS companion
· Home Assistant integration through MQTT discovery
· System health: the app tells you when the camera or disk needs attention
· One-file backup and restore of your whole setup
· Seven languages: English, Hungarian, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean
  and Chinese

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install the free server on a computer at home (one-line script).
2. Prop up an old phone running PetTrack in monitor mode — that's the camera.
3. Install this app on your everyday phone, scan the pairing code, done.

Everything works on your home Wi-Fi. Want access from outside? Point the
app at your Tailscale address — the docs cover it in one paragraph.

PRIVACY

Self-hosted means self-hosted: video, events and pet health data live on
your own server. The only optional third-party service is ntfy for remote
alerts, and it stays off unless you turn it on. Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/szbnce/PetTrack/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md
